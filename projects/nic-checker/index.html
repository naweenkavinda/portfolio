Check digit working

Old 10-character NICs use weights 3 2 7 6 5 4 3 2 on the first eight digits. 12-digit NICs use weights 8 4 3 2 7 6 5 8 4 3 2 on the first eleven, which gives the same check digit for numbers converted from the old format. Both were worked out from known NICs and haven't been officially published. The weight on the first serial digit hasn't yet been tested on a real NIC whose serial is 1000 or higher. A correct check digit means a number is well-formed, not that it was issued. Day numbers count February as 29 days in every year.
